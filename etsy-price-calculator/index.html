How much does Etsy take per sale?

For a US shop, Etsy charges four kinds of fees on a sale. These are the same rates the calculator uses by default.

Listing fee
$0.20
Transaction fee (item price + shipping + gift wrap)
6.5%
Payment processing (whole order total)
3% + $0.25
Offsite Ads (only on orders that come from one)
15% or 12%

The calculator adds these up as one percentage (6.5% + 3% = 9.5%) and one fixed fee ($0.20 + $0.25 = $0.45). Offsite Ads only apply to some orders, so it sets aside 3% for them instead of the full 15%.

$0.20

$1.30

$0.85

$2.35
Offsite Ads set-aside: 3% × $20
$0.60

In the calculator's formula that is $20 × (9.5% + 3%) + $0.45 = $2.95 in fees. If that sale did come from an Offsite Ad, Etsy would take 15% ($3.00) on top of the $2.35.

How this calculator works out your price

It adds up what one sale costs you: materials, your time, packaging, shipping, overhead and any ad spend per sale, plus Etsy's $0.45 in fixed fees. It then divides that by what is left of each dollar after Etsy's percentage fees, the Offsite Ads set-aside, your refund allowance and the profit margin you want. If you charge buyers for shipping or run discounts, it adjusts for those too. The result is the lowest price that still leaves your target margin after every fee.

Price = (costs + fixed fees) ÷ (1 − percentage fees − refunds − margin)

Example: a wax melt pack costs $10.39 to make and ship. With $0.45 in fixed fees, 12.5% in fees, a 2% refund allowance and a 30% margin: (10.39 + 0.45) ÷ 0.555 = $19.53.

Frequently asked questions

Does Etsy charge fees on shipping?

Yes. The 6.5% transaction fee applies to the shipping you charge the buyer, and the 3% + $0.25 payment processing fee applies to the whole order total, shipping included. The calculator counts shipping charged to the buyer in the order total for this reason.

What are offsite ads fees?

Etsy advertises listings on other sites, such as social networks and search engines. If a buyer clicks one of those ads and orders from your shop within 30 days, Etsy charges 15% of the order total, or 12% once your shop has made $10,000 or more in sales in the past 365 days. The fee is capped at $100 per order. Shops under $10,000 can opt out.

Is the listing fee charged again when an item sells?

Often, yes. If a listing has more than one item in stock, Etsy renews it for another $0.20 each time one sells. Listings also expire after four months and renew automatically for $0.20 unless you turn that off. The calculator counts $0.20 on every sale so this cost is covered.

How do I price to make a profit on Etsy?

Add up everything one sale costs you, including your time, add Etsy's fixed fees, then divide by what is left after Etsy's percentage fees, refunds and the margin you want. Enter your costs in the calculator above and it does this for you.

Are these fees the same outside the US?

No. The $0.20 listing fee and 6.5% transaction fee apply everywhere, but payment processing depends on where your bank account is (for example 4% + £0.20 in the UK and 4% + €0.30 in most EU countries). Some countries also add a regulatory operating fee, and VAT may be added to Etsy's fees. Change "Fee %" and "Fixed fee" in the settings to match your country.

Source: Etsy's fee page. Rates checked on 4 November 2026.

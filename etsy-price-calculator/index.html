How to price a product on Etsy

Start from your costs, not from a guess. Add up what one sale costs you: materials, your time, packaging, shipping and a share of overhead. Add Etsy's fixed fees. Then divide by what's left after Etsy's percentage fees and your profit margin.

Price = (costs + fixed fees) ÷ (1 − percentage fees − margin)

Example: a wax melt pack costs $10.39 to make and ship. With $0.45 in fixed fees, 14.5% in percentage fees and a 30% margin: (10.39 + 0.45) ÷ 0.555 = $19.53. Sold at $10, it loses $2.29 on every sale. At $19.53 it keeps $5.86.

Frequently asked questions

What fees does Etsy charge in the US?

A $0.20 listing fee, a 6.5% transaction fee on the item price plus shipping and gift wrap, and payment processing of 3% + $0.25 on the order total. Offsite Ads take 15% of an order that comes from an Etsy ad on another site, or 12% once a shop has made $10,000 in the last 12 months, capped at $100 per order.

Does Etsy charge fees on shipping?

Yes. The 6.5% transaction fee also applies to the shipping you charge, and payment processing applies to the whole order total.

Why does the calculator add a buffer for ads and refunds?

Some sales come through Offsite Ads, and some orders get refunded. A small buffer keeps those sales from turning into losses.

What profit margin should I use?

That's your choice. The example uses 30%, which means 30 cents of every dollar is profit after all costs and fees.

Is the calculator free? Is my data saved?

Yes, it's free. Your numbers are saved only in your own browser and never leave your device.

Do these rates apply outside the US?

No. Fees differ by country. Change the settings to your country's rates using Etsy's Fees & Payments Policy.

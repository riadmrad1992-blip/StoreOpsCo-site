Etsy's new terms start December 16. Here are the 4 rules that apply to every shop.

Most of the talk is about the AI training clause. But section 6 of the same terms adds rules about your tools, hidden text and AI that can cost you your shop. Every rule below links to Etsy's own Terms of Use.

In one line

The AI training clause gets the headlines, but section 6 of the new terms adds rules that apply to every shop: no instructions aimed at Etsy's systems, you answer for every tool you connect, and no Etsy content in AI prompts.

What changed about AI training

  • The October version let Etsy and its third-party partners and vendors use your content to develop, train, test and improve AI and machine learning.
  • On November 23 Etsy added: "We do not use your content to train AI models designed to copy your original work or replace your creativity."
  • What didn't change: the license is still perpetual and irrevocable, and Etsy's privacy policy doesn't describe a clear opt-out for US sellers. Closing your shop may not take back what you already posted.

The 4 rules that apply to every shop

Section What it says What it means for you
6F Don't Manipulate Our Systems No instructions in listings, descriptions, images or messages meant to make Etsy's systems behave in a way Etsy didn't intend, hidden or visible. Prompt injection and jailbreaking are named. "Violations of this Section may result in immediate termination of your account." Don't add "AI, ignore this image" text to photos or listings.
6C Automated access Automated access counts as using Etsy. Whoever deploys, controls or benefits from an automated agent is responsible for that agent's use of the Services. Every extension, bot, script and AI agent on your shop is your responsibility.
6E Don't Steal Our Stuff No using content or data from Etsy to develop, train, test or prompt an AI system without Etsy's express permission. Don't paste other shops' listings or Etsy pages into a chatbot.
6D Etsy's AI tools "You are responsible for reviewing, evaluating, and determining the appropriateness of any AI-generated output." Read every AI-suggested description or reply before it goes out.

What to do before December 16

Step Do this Time
1 Write a list of every tool that touches your shop: research extensions, relisters, message bots, AI assistants. 10 min
2 Keep tools that connect through Etsy's "Allow access" screen. Remove or question anything that asks for your password or scrapes pages. 15 min
3 Search your listings and photos for any text aimed at AI or Etsy's systems. Remove it. 20 min
4 Using an image-protection ("poisoning") tool? Etsy's terms don't name these tools either way. Ask Etsy support in writing first and keep the reply. 5 min
5 Save your own titles, descriptions and photos outside Etsy. Use only those files with AI tools. 30 min
6 Read section 6 of the terms yourself. 15 min

What not to do

  • Don't close your shop to "undo" the AI license. It may not take back what you posted. If you leave, leave for business reasons.
  • Don't trust summaries (including this one) over the terms themselves. Etsy can update them again.
  • Don't assume a tool is safe because it's popular. You carry the responsibility, not the tool maker.

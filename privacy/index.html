Privacy policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Who we are

StoreOpsCo makes free tools and videos about the back office of selling on Etsy. It is run by Riad Mrad. Questions about this policy: riad.mrad1992@gmail.com.

The Etsy price calculator

The numbers you type into the calculator stay in your browser. They are saved only on your device so they are there next time, and they are never sent to us or anyone else. You can clear them at any time by clearing the calculator or your browser data.

The page loads its font from Google Fonts. The video's preview picture loads from YouTube's image server; the video itself loads from YouTube only if you press play.

The email sign-up

The email sign-up on this site is handled by Kit (kit.com), our email provider. When you sign up, Kit stores your email address so we can send you the emails you signed up for, such as the weekly Etsy pricing breakdown.

  • You only get the emails you signed up for. We do not sell or share your address.
  • You can unsubscribe from any email: every email has an unsubscribe link at the bottom.
  • You can also ask us to delete your address at any time.

YouTube

The homepage shows preview pictures of our latest videos, loaded from YouTube's image server. Pressing one opens the video on YouTube.

StoreOpsCo uses the YouTube Data API only to upload and schedule videos to our own YouTube channel. We do not access, collect, store or share data from any other YouTube user.

What we don't do

No ads, no tracking cookies, and no selling of data.

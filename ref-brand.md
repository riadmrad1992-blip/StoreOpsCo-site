# Brand for the website

## The idea
**Chaos snaps into one connected system.** Shop work (messages, refunds, fees, guesswork) becomes calm, ordered and lit. Four products are four *engines* stacked into one system.

| Engine | Product | Colour |
|---|---|---|
| 01 Communicate | Customer Response Bank | `#7CB7FF` |
| 02 Resolve | Refund & Dispute Manager | `#E5484D` |
| 03 Know your profit | Profit Pilot | `#2FBF71` |
| 04 Automate | AI Assistant (in the Back Office + AI bundle) | `#F27A2E` |

## Colours
ground `#0E141B` · ground-deep `#0A0E13` · surface `#151D27` · surface-raised `#1B2531` · line `#26313D` · ink `#F3F1EC` · ink-muted `#8C97A3` · signal `#F27A2E` · on-signal `#0E141B` · ledger `#2FBF71` · loss `#FF6B70` · caution `#F5A524`. Scene-only: paper `#F3F1EC`, kraft `#C89B6D`, coin `#F2B33D`.

Page background: vertical gradient ground-deep to ground, one soft lighter-blue highlight at the top, one faint orange glow in a lower corner, hairline grid at about 3.5% opacity. Cards: gradient surface-raised to surface, 1px line border, radius 18px, shadow `0 24px 50px -24px rgba(0,0,0,.8)`. **One glow per view:** only the single most important thing on screen gets `text-shadow: 0 0 40px rgba(242,122,46,.45)`.

## Type
Plus Jakarta Sans 800 for headings and numbers, tight tracking. Inter 500/600/700 for text. Body 16px on the site (15px minimum). Kickers: uppercase, 12–13px, 0.14em tracking, muted. Tabular figures for numbers. Google Fonts with `display=swap` and real fallback stacks.

## Shape and controls
Radius 8 (inputs), 14 (tiles), 18 (cards), 999 (pills, buttons). Buttons are pills: primary = signal fill with `on-signal` text; secondary = surface with a line border. Focus ring: 3px signal outline with 2px offset. Tap targets at least 44px.

## Voice
A calm operator who has done the maths for you. Plain words, short sentences, real numbers. No hype ("game-changer", "unlock", "boost"), no fear. Lead with the number.

Approved lines: "The back office for small online shops." · "Chaos snaps into one connected system." · "Every hidden cost. Counted." · "Know your real profit on every sale." · "Your time is a cost." · "Check the math."

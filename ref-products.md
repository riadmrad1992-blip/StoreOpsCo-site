# Product facts (the only source for page copy)

The Etsy shop is **not open yet**. All prices below are the planned prices; do not show a price for anything marked "no price".

## Profit Pilot: $17 (standalone, the first product to launch)
A Notion template that shows the **real profit on every product** after materials, your time, packaging, shipping, overhead, Etsy fees, ads, refunds and marketing. It gives a **recommended price** and a **Health** status for each product (Losing money, Below target, Healthy). Views: Profit Leaks (products losing money), Best Earners, and a list of material prices to re-check. Example used in the videos: a $10 wax melt pack loses $2.29 per sale; at the recommended $19.53 it earns $5.86 (30% margin). US Etsy rates 2026.

## Customer Response Bank: $12 (standalone)
A Notion page of ready-made replies for the messages every Etsy seller gets: shipping, custom orders, refunds, disputes and reviews, with a Formal or Friendly tone for each. Copy, change one detail, send.

## Refund & Dispute Manager (sold only inside bundles, never alone)
A Notion log for refunds and cases: reason, amount, status and outcome, so the pattern shows up and the cause can be fixed.

## Back Office + AI: $39 (flagship bundle)
Profit Pilot, Customer Response Bank, Refund & Dispute Manager and an AI Assistant page, together.

## Customer Service Kit (bundle): no price
Customer Response Bank and Refund & Dispute Manager together. **Do not show a price.**
